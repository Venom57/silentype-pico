# Silentype Connector Signal Map — DB-9 (DEFINITIVE)

Source: Apple KB TA27850 + printer schematic 050-0023-01 (`docs/firmware-archaeology/`).
The earlier 26-pin template was wrong — that ribbon is internal to the Apple II card.

| DB-9 pin | Name | Dir (Pico view) | Printer side | Carrier net | Pico GPIO |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shield | — | NC | chassis | — |
| 2 | +5 V | → | 74LS673/74LS00/ULN logic/555 | +5V rail | — |
| 3 | Logic GND | — | | GND | — |
| 4 | SD — Serial Data | → | 74LS673 I/O (pin 6) | SD_5V | GP2 via U2 |
| 5 | MS — Machine Status | ← | margin switch to GND; open = high | MS (10k pull-up to 3V3) | GP5 |
| 6 | +12 V | → | head (via ULN2003), both motors | VMOT = **+12 V** | — |
| 7 | Power GND | — | | GND (star) | — |
| 8 | XC — Store Clock | → | 74LS673 ST CK (↑ latches); SC=XC=1 → ST CLR̅ (all off) | XC_5V | GP4 via U2 |
| 9 | SC — Shift Clock | → | 74LS00 inverter → 74LS673 SH̅ C̅K̅ (SC ↑ shifts) | SC_5V | GP3 via U2 |

Levels: SC/XC have 3 kΩ pull-ups to +5 V inside the printer and are 74LS inputs. Drive
them through a 74AHCT125 (3 channels) or '245. MS is an open switch to ground — pull up
on our side to 3V3; **do not** let +5 V onto a Pico pin.

Shift word: word bit N = latch output YN. **Bit 15 is shifted first** (the 74LS673's first-in
bit ends at Y15), exactly as the Apple card ROM sends head bit 6 first. `[3:0]` carriage phases,
`[7:4]` paper phases, `[8]` R/W̅ = 0 always, `[15:9]` head elements Y09 (bottom) … Y15 (top).
Outputs change only on the XC rising edge, which must happen with **SC low**: SC and XC high
together assert the printer's ST CLR̅ and zero all 16 outputs (that is also what the printer's
555 head-on watchdog does after ≈5-6 ms of continuous burn — motors included).

Per word (all generated by PIO, `firmware/src/silentype_sr.pio`; details and evidence in
`docs/firmware-archaeology/HARDWARE-INTERFACE.md` §2):

```
idle  SC=1 XC=0 SD=0
16×   SD=bit while SC=1 (1 µs) → SC=0 (1 µs) → SC=1 = shift edge (0.5 µs)     MSB first
store SD=0 SC=0 (2 µs) → XC=1 = latch edge (2 µs) → XC=0 (2 µs) → SC=1 → idle   ≈47.5 µs total
```

GP3 (SC) and GP4 (XC) must stay adjacent — they are one 2-pin PIO side-set. While the Pico
is in reset its pins float: pull SC/XC up and SD down at the 74AHCT125 inputs so the printer
sees SC=XC=1 (= clear, all outputs off), as the Apple card presents on reset.

Power budget: head is 7 × element through ULN2003 at 12 V — measure element R to size
the 12 V supply; original Apple II PSU 12 V rail is ~1.5 A total. The /// used its own
12 V. Start with a 12 V / 2 A bench supply.
